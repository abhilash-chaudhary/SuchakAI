# SuchakAI (सूचक AI)

**SuchakAI** is an intelligent civic-tech platform designed to bridge the gap between complex government schemes and Indian citizens. Across India, thousands of Central and State welfare programs remain underutilized due to fragmented information, convoluted eligibility rules, and opaque application processes. SuchakAI makes welfare discovery and access transparent, accurate, and effortless.

---

## What SuchakAI Does

* **Smart Eligibility Matching**: Uses a deterministic 6-factor matching engine that evaluates citizen profiles (age, state, category, occupation, income, and demographics) against official government scheme rules without hallucinations.
* **AI-Powered Explanations**: Integrates Google Gemini AI to translate bureaucratic scheme guidelines into plain-language summaries explaining exactly why a citizen qualifies, what documents they need, and what pitfalls to avoid.
* **Interactive AI Civic Assistant**: A conversational chatbot that guides citizens through scheme discovery, required checklists, application procedures, and direct portal links.
* **Curated Scheme Directory**: A searchable, filterable repository of Central and State schemes spanning Education, Agriculture, Healthcare, Entrepreneurship, Skill Development, and Social Welfare.
* **Multi-User & Guest Support**: Allows citizens to sign up and save their profiles and bookmarked schemes securely, while also offering a frictionless guest mode for immediate access.

---

## Mission

To ensure that every eligible citizen receives the welfare benefits, subsidies, and scholarships they are entitled to—empowering communities through technology and transparency.
